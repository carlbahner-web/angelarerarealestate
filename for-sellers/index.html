For sellers

Five questions before you list your house.

Most people start by asking what it's worth. I'd rather start here, because the right number depends on nearly every one of these.

There are no wrong answers and nothing is being scored. Pick whatever is closest to true, and I'll tell you what it means for your sale.

Question 1 of 5

What happens if it doesn't sell?

Why I ask: this tells me more about how to price it, and how patient we can afford to be, than almost anything else.

Are you buying at the same time?

Why I ask: this is the part people put off thinking about, and it is the part with the most moving pieces.

Where did your number come from?

Why I ask: I am not asking whether your number is right. I am asking where it came from, because that tells me which conversation the two of us need to have.

How much are you willing to do before it lists?

Why I ask: of everything on this page, this is the one that most reliably changes what the house sells for.

What would make you take an offer that isn't the highest?

Why I ask: the best offer is not always the biggest one, and it helps to know how you feel about that before they start arriving.
