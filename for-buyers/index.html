For buyers

Five questions before you see a single house.

Most people start by looking at houses. I'd rather start here, because nearly every hard decision later traces back to one of these five.

There are no wrong answers and nothing is being scored. Pick whatever is closest to true, and I'll tell you what it means for your search.

Question 1 of 5

How long do you actually plan to live there?

Why I ask: this changes which houses are worth your time more than almost anything else on your list.

Who gets the final say?

Why I ask: I have watched this stall more searches than money ever has.

How much of the work are you honestly willing to take on?

Why I ask: not what you would like to be true about yourself. What you will actually do on a Saturday in November.

What is left over after closing?

Why I ask: this is the number nobody plans for. Down payment, closing costs, and then you are standing in an empty house.

If you had to let one of these go, which would it be?

Why I ask: everybody's list is longer than their budget. Knowing what you would trade away is worth more than knowing what you want.
